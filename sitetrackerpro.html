What it does

Six things that decide whether a project makes money

Most site software records what happened. SiteTrackerPro is built around the numbers that get argued over at the end of the job — volumes, quantities, deliveries and progress.

Surveying & earthworks

Enter control points, spot elevations and the building footprint. The platform returns excavation and fill volumes plus the material quantities implied by them — no separate spreadsheet, no hand transpose.

Cut & fillControl pointsElevations

3D BIM viewer

Open the model in the browser and walk around it. Rotate, pan and zoom, or cut a cross-section to check a level against the design. Tablet-ready for site use.

Materials & bills of quantities

Eighty-plus Ethiopian standard materials across seven categories — foundation, structure, masonry, MEP, finishes, roofing and safety. Quantities generate from the building size, and you can add your own local supplier items.

Auto quantitiesBOMCustom items

Delivery fraud checks

Every material delivery is scored against quantity, timing and value, with the weighting shown rather than hidden. You get a defensible flag — not an accusation, and not a blind acceptance.

Why we built it

The gap between the survey and the site report

We survey sites, so we kept watching the same failure: accurate field data going into a project, then being re-typed, rounded and argued over until nobody trusted the number.

SiteTrackerPro closes that gap. The volumes come from the same coordinates our crews capture, the quantities come from those volumes, and the progress you report is the progress the platform recorded — traceable back to the point it came from.

It works standalone on your projects, or with our survey team feeding it directly. Either way, it is configured for Ethiopian construction practice, with local standard materials already loaded.

Can we use it without your survey team?

Yes. SiteTrackerPro stands on its own — you enter control points and progress yourselves. Working with our crews simply means the data arrives already captured and checked.

Where does it run?

It runs in any modern browser. We can host it for you, or deploy it on your own server with your own database, so the records stay under your control.

What does it cost?

It is quoted per project or per organisation after a scoping call, because the set-up differs — number of sites, users and whether rates and materials need loading. No price list is published before we understand the scope.

Does it replace our accounting system?

No. It tracks construction quantities, progress and delivery records. It is designed to feed your existing reporting, not to be a general ledger.
